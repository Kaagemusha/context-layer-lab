Try to fool the checker.

The answer is never read from the file. The checker recomputes it from the run receipts and rejects a snapshot that disagrees with itself. Pick an edit. The page applies it to a copy of the public snapshot and runs the same checker.

These edits use the public scenario. Go back to it to try them.

The limit: if someone rewrites the receipts, their claims, and the answer together, the checker will agree with them. It checks that a snapshot is consistent, not that its sources are true.

Check your own snapshot.

Generate a snapshot from your own records and open it here. It is read in this tab only and checked the same way. A snapshot that does not match its own evidence is rejected, and the page keeps its previous answer.

    What this proves, and what it does not.

    It proves the records you can open support the answer, under explicit freshness and schedule rules. The answer is recomputed from them in your browser, and a snapshot that disagrees with itself is rejected.

    It does not prove that a source is true, give anyone permission to act, or fill in what the records leave out. Missing evidence is reported as missing.
